Hi! I am a PhD student in the Department of Sociology at Harvard University and a Doctoral Trainee at the Harvard Center for Population and Development Studies. I primarily use large-scale data and quantitative methods to study economic inequalities, with a focus on the roles of education, demographic changes, and the labor market. I also have a keen interest in understanding how social policies can mitigate inequalities. In my current project, I am investigating how changes in the distributions and wage returns to different fields of study have shaped the gender wage gap in the United States. My collaborative work examines economic returns to "some college", mechanisms through which secondary schools contribute to SES achievement gaps, as well as the joint effects of educational attainment and family structure on trends in income inequality.

You can find my CV [here](https://jenchenchao.github.io/files/cv_jchao_2025july.pdf).

I will be presenting at [RC28](https://rc28.soc.ucla.edu/) in LA and [ASA](https://www.asanet.org/2025-annual-meeting/) in Chicago. Would love to meet and chat!
